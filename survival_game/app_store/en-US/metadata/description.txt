Survive the jungle, build your camp, and find a way home. Lost Canopy is an offline survival adventure where every expedition starts with a choice: push deeper for supplies, or return while you still can.

Plan each day around limited daylight. Keep your health, water, food and sanity under control as you forage, craft and explore. Dangerous wildlife, toxic food and harsh weather can turn a promising trip into a fight to get home.

- Upgrade your shelter from a basic refuge to a rescue base.
- Craft tools and weapons, and repair worn equipment.
- Explore riverbanks, bamboo groves, ancient ruins and a plane wreck.
- Face wildlife encounters through choices that use your gear and supplies.
- Catch different fish with timing-based controls, and prepare hazardous catches before eating.
- Gather wood, forage and hunt in hands-on minigames.
- Learn which foods are safe and manage poison, injuries and exposure.
- Pack supplies for each expedition and bring your finds back to camp.

Survival is only the beginning. Recover the missing beacon parts, solve the ancient stone puzzle, send a rescue signal and survive the final storm.

Take your time when reading events: daylight does not pass while you decide. Your journey saves automatically, so you can return to an unfinished expedition.

Play solo, including offline. How far will you push before turning back?
